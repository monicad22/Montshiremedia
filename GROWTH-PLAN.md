# 30–60–90 day SEO and conversion plan

## Days 1–30: establish a reliable baseline

Complete the launch checklist first. Connect Search Console, consent-aware analytics and CRM attribution. Define a qualified lead with the founder, distinguish enquiry from booking and measure each step. Audit crawling, indexation, mobile usability and core journeys. Map service pages to intent without creating near-duplicate location pages. Publish the reviewed SEO foundation article. Establish a real Vancouver business presence only where eligible. Record weekly baseline visibility, qualified enquiries and conversion rate; avoid arbitrary performance promises.

## Days 31–60: build relevant evidence

Review search queries and lead quality. Improve page titles and internal links where impressions indicate demand. Publish the reviewed paid-media and performance articles with original evidence. Add the first approved case study with dated, attributable metrics. Interview prospects about uncertainty in the proposal process and revise service-page FAQs. Test one CTA or form-friction hypothesis at a time; use qualitative feedback where traffic is insufficient for a meaningful experiment.

## Days 61–90: invest in what converts

Compare qualified pipeline by landing page and channel, accounting for sales-cycle lag. Expand the strongest topic cluster with genuinely useful content, not thin keyword variants. Earn relevant links through partnerships and original expertise. Prioritize slow mobile journeys using field data. Assess booked-to-qualified-call and proposal progression, then update the next quarterly roadmap. Scale paid tests only after conversion quality and economics are understood.

## Operating rhythm

Weekly: tracking health, form delivery, search errors and campaign anomalies. Monthly: lead quality, content contribution, experiment decisions and technical performance. Quarterly: channel mix, capacity, client proof and positioning. Set numerical targets from actual baseline data and business economics, rather than invented benchmarks.

## Updated launch gates and review-offer measurement

The 28-page upgrade adds a free SEO/site health review, engagement models, a manifesto and implementation-specific trust statements. Start the 30-day clock only after real form delivery, consent handling, owner review and the existing Sites deployment are verified.

Days 1–30: validate the review-offer scope and fulfilment capacity. Measure CTA → submitted request → qualified review → booked conversation as separate steps. A browser download is not a lead, and an SMTP acceptance is not a booked call. Check submitted enquiries against actual inbox receipt. Configure GTM/GA4 IDs only after tag/privacy review; test denial and withdrawal before collecting data.

Days 31–60: review the questions and recurring issues in delivered site reviews, using consented, anonymized themes for content. Improve the relevant industry-to-service links and service FAQs instead of adding thin combinations of every keyword and industry. Review engagement-page questions with real prospects.

Days 61–90: compare review requests with qualified opportunities and delivery effort. Refine eligibility and capacity based on actual workload. Publish approved evidence in the structured case-study collection and expand articles through the content files. Re-measure production performance after any provider scripts are added. Keep Security and Accessibility statements synchronized with what has actually been deployed and tested.
